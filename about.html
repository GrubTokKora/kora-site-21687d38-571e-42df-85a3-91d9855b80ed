About Newport Sailing School and Tours

Newport, RI's first and only pure sailing tour — family owned and operated for more than 50 years.

Our Story

Newport's First and Only Pure Sailing Tour

Newport Sailing School and Tours is Newport, Rhode Island's first and only pure sailing tour. For more than 50 years, we have been introducing people to the joy and art of "pure" sailing. As a local family owned business we work to share our love for Newport and our passion for sailing with everyone who sets sail with us. We pride ourselves in providing the highest quality sailing experiences and excellent customer service.

We are a proud family owned and operated business established in 1965, striving to continue our legacy of excellent instruction and sailing tours for years to come. Captain Mike and family have welcomed thousands of guests aboard — pointing out landmarks, recommending favourite local restaurants, and making every sail feel personal. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a returning friend, you'll always find a warm welcome at Dock A5.

Family owned and operated since 1965 · ASA Certified

Our Mission

Committed to Safe, Memorable Sailing

Here at Newport Sailing School and Tours, we are proud to be leaders in the local sailing industry. We have introduced thousands of people to the art of "pure sailing" already, and continually strive to share that experience with everyone who joins us for a sailing course or tour. We are committed to providing safe, enjoyable, informative, and memorable experiences for everyone who sets sail with us, and pride ourselves in our high quality customer service.

Why Sail With Us

What Sets Us Apart

Experienced Local Crew

For over 50+ years, Newport Sailing School and Tours has been providing the finest sailing instruction and tours in Newport Harbor and Narragansett Bay.

ASA Certified

We are proud to be Certified by the American Sailing Association, and to offer ASA keelboat instruction courses for all levels of experience.

Unforgettable Scenery

Discover spectacular Newport Harbor, Castle Hill, Jamestown, Hammersmith Farm, Clingstone, and breathtaking sunsets from the water.
