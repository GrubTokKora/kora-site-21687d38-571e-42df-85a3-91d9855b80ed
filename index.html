Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the sailing tours depart from?

All cruises depart from the beautiful Goat Island Marina in downtown Newport, RI — Dock A5, 5 Marina Plaza, next to Newport Harbor Island Resort. Free parking is available at our kiosk.

How long are the sailing tours?

Day sails run approximately 75 minutes and sunset sails run approximately 90 minutes. Sailing cruises depart every 90 minutes throughout the day from May through November.

How many people can come aboard?

Our private sails accommodate up to 6 guests. Group sails are booked per person. Extended tours and larger group charters can also be arranged — just give us a call.

Do I need sailing experience to join a tour?

Not at all. Our experienced local crew handles everything on a narrated tour. If you'd like to take the helm, ask about our ASA-certified sailing instruction for all experience levels.

What is your cancellation policy?

All bookings are non-refundable — no refunds, no exchanges, no exclusions. Rain checks are issued for severe or inclement weather at the discretion of Newport Sailing School and Tours on the day of your scheduled tour.
