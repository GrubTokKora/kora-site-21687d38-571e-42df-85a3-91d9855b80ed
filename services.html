Sailing Tours

Newport Harbor & Narragansett Bay

Relax and enjoy a scenic, narrated sailing tour of Newport Harbor and Narragansett Bay. Our daily sailing tours depart every 90 minutes from Goat Island Marina and take you sailing past Newport's charming waterfront, seaside estates, Fort Adams, Castle Hill, Clingstone, Jamestown and breathtaking lighthouses — all from the deck of a real sailboat, under sail.

Sailing Instruction

Learn to Sail in Newport, RI

Ready to take the helm? Newport Sailing School is a proud local leader in sailing instruction since 1971. Our courses are designed to provide the ultimate sailing instruction in the shortest period of time — whether you're a complete beginner or looking to earn your ASA certification. All lessons depart from Goat Island Marina in downtown Newport.
