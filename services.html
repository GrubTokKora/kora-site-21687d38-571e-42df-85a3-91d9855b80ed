Our Sailing Services

Learn more about the different sailing experiences available at Newport Sailing School and Tours.

Sailing Tours

Relax and enjoy a scenic sailing tour of Newport Harbor and Narragansett Bay. Our daily sailing tours depart every 90 minutes from Goat Island Marina and take you past Newport's charming waterfront, yachts, seaside estates, Fort Adams, lighthouses, and many other spectacular sights.

Learn More

Sailing Instruction

Ready to take the helm? Learn to sail with the finest in sailing instruction through our successful basic, intermediate, or advanced courses. Each course is designed to provide the ultimate sailing instruction in the shortest period of time.

Learn More

Team Building

Newport Sailing has specialized in corporate charters and team building events for over 35 years. Let us plan your next corporate adventure — a harbor tour, an intro sailing lesson followed by match or fleet racing, or a completely custom experience your team will love.

Learn More

Custom Sailing Experience

Enjoy a private sailing adventure custom tailored to your unique needs. Whether it's a romantic engagement, a bachelor or bachelorette party, a special anniversary, a family adventure, or any other occasion, we'll help create a sailing experience you'll always remember.
